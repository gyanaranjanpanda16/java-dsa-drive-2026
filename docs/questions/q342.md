# Q342 — Find minimum dice rolls on a board with snakes and ladders

**Topic:** Graphs · **Level:** Medium

## Question and contract

Find minimum dice rolls on a board with snakes and ladders. Implement the operation exposed by `int solve(int[] jump)`. Nonempty jump array; -1 means no jump, otherwise a valid destination index. Start at0; die outcomes1..6; exact landing required; apply one jump per roll, including on the final square.

## Runnable example

```java
solve(new int[]{-1,-1,8,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1})
```

Expected output:

```text
2
```

## How and why the answer works

Board squares are states and each die outcome is an edge of cost one after its forced jump. BFS therefore finds the fewest rolls even when snakes form cycles. The visited array prevents looping; ArrayDeque supplies the FIFO frontier.

## Walkthrough

The board has a ladder from cell 2 to 8. From start 0, roll 2 to land on 2 and immediately move to 8; roll 1 to reach destination 9. BFS therefore finds 2 throws. A die roll plus its resulting ladder or snake counts as one transition, not two throws.

## Complexity and tradeoffs

O(N) time because each square has at most six outgoing moves; O(N) space. This cost describes the supplied representation and stated input contract. A more general version may need extra validation or a different representation; do not silently extend the assumptions.

## Checks and edge cases

- A ladder accelerates the route.
- Already at the destination.
- Every legal landing returns to the start.
