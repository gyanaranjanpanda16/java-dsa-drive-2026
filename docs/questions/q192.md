# Q192 — Compute consecutive stock-price spans

**Topic:** Stacks, queues, and deques · **Level:** Medium

## Question and contract

For each price return the count of consecutive days ending today whose prices are at most today, including today.

## Example

Input:[100,80,60,70,60,75,85].

Expected printed output:

```text
[1, 1, 1, 2, 1, 4, 6]
```

## Approach and why each part exists

A direct scan or simulation establishes the baseline; the implementation below chooses the operations required by the contract.

After removing earlier prices at most current, the top is the nearest strictly higher barrier. The distance to that barrier is exactly the valid consecutive span.
