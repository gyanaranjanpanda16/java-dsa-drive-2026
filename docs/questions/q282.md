# Q282 — Find minimum root-to-leaf depth in nodes

**Topic:** Trees and BST · **Level:** Intermediate

## Question and contract

## How and why the answer works

The minimum must range over real leaf paths. Treating a missing child as a depth-zero candidate would incorrectly stop early at one-child nodes.
