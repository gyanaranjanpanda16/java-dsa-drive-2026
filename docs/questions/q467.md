# Q467 — Minimum Rescue Boats

**Topic:** Greedy and search on answer · **Difficulty:** Medium

## Question and contract

Implement `Minimum Rescue Boats` using the operation demonstrated below. At most two people per boat, total weight at most limit; return-1 if an individual exceeds it.

## Demonstration

```java
minimum(new int[]{3,2,2,1},3)
```

Expected output:

```text
3
```

## How and why

The heaviest remaining person must take a boat. If the lightest cannot accompany them, nobody can; otherwise pairing with the lightest can be exchanged into an optimal solution without adding boats.

The source includes comments at each meaningful line. Start by naming the state, identify the stopping or initialization case, and then follow how a smaller solved state or an accepted choice contributes to the answer. Do not memorize the loop order without the state definition.

## Walkthrough

For1,2,2,3 and limit3, send3 alone,2+1 together, and the other2 alone: three boats.

## Complexity and tradeoffs

O(n log n) time and O(n) copied space.
