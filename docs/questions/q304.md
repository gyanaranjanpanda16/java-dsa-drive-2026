# Q304 — Minimize refueling stops to reach a target

**Topic:** Greedy max-heap · **Level:** Hard

## Question and contract

Minimize refueling stops to reach a target. Implement the operation exposed by `int solve(int target,int startFuel,int[][] stations)`. Nonnegative target and start fuel; stations sorted by position with nonnegative positions/fuel; fuel consumption is one unit per distance, tank capacity unlimited.

## Runnable example

```java
solve(100,10,new int[][]{{10,60},{20,30},{30,30},{60,40}})
```

Expected output:

```text
2
```

## How and why the answer works

Delay committing to a refill until progress requires it. Among previously reachable stations, choosing the largest supply gives at least as much future reach as any other single stop; exchanging another choice for this one cannot hurt feasibility.

O(n log(n+1)) time and O(n) heap space. This cost describes the supplied representation and stated input contract. A more general version may need extra validation or a different representation; do not silently extend the assumptions.
