# Q150 — Find a longest palindromic substring

**Topic:** Strings · **Level:** Medium

## Question and contract

Return the earliest longest palindromic substring under UTF-16 char equality; empty text returns empty text.

## Example

Input: babad.

Expected printed output:

```text
bab
```

## Approach and why each part exists

A direct scan or simulation establishes the baseline; the implementation below chooses the operations required by the contract.

Every palindrome has a center at a character or between characters. Expanding each center finds its largest palindrome; comparing all centers therefore includes an optimal answer.

Center at index1 expands to bab; center2 yields aba of equal length and therefore does not replace the earlier result.
