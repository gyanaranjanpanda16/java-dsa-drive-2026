## How and why the answer works

For nonnegative distances, squaring preserves comparisons. The queue repeatedly exposes the closest remaining point; coordinate ties make the example deterministic without altering the distance criterion.

## Walkthrough

Squared distances for (3,3),(1,1),(-1,0) are 18,2,1. The two nearest points are therefore (-1,0) then(1,1). Squaring avoids an unnecessary square root because square root preserves order for nonnegative distances. The source's numeric contract determines how safely coordinates can be squared.
