# Q290 — Minimize the cost of connecting ropes

**Topic:** Heaps and greedy · **Level:** Medium

## Question and contract

Minimize the cost of connecting ropes. Implement the operation exposed by `long solve(int[] lengths)`. Nonnegative lengths; exact intermediate sums must fit long.

## Runnable example

```java
solve(new int[]{4,3,2,6})
```

Expected output:

```text
29
```

## How and why the answer works

A rope contributes its length once for every ancestor merge. In an optimal binary merge tree, two smallest weights can be siblings at greatest depth; exchanging larger leaves with them cannot increase cost. Contracting those siblings reduces to the same problem, justifying repeated smallest-pair merging.
