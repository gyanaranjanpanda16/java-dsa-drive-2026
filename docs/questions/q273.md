## How and why the answer works

A node must satisfy bounds inherited from all ancestors, not just its parent. Narrowing the upper bound on the left and lower bound on the right enforces the full BST invariant.

## Walkthrough

The sample tree has root `4`, children `2` and `6`, and leaf pairs `1,3` below `2` and `5,7` below `6`.

Root 4 permits only values below 4 in its left subtree and above 4 in its right subtree. Descending to 2 tightens the allowed range for its right child 3 to greater than 2 and less than 4. Every sample node fits its inherited bounds. Comparing only each node with its immediate children would miss violations of an earlier ancestor's bound.
