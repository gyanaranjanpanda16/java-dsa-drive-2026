# Q160 — Find starts containing a permutation of equal-length words

**Topic:** Strings · **Level:** Hard

## Question and contract

Words are nonempty and have equal positive char length. Return all starts whose fixed-size substring is a concatenation of every word once, including duplicate multiplicities. Empty word array returns no starts.

## Example

Input:barfoothefoobarman and[foo,bar].

Expected printed output:

```text
[0, 9]
```

## Approach and why each part exists

A direct scan or simulation establishes the baseline; the implementation below chooses the operations required by the contract.

At a candidate start, aligned chunks partition the whole candidate substring. Matching their frequency map to required multiplicities proves every required word occurs exactly once per required copy.

## Walkthrough

At0 chunks bar,foo satisfy both quotas. At9 chunks foo,bar also satisfy them; other starts contain unknown chunks.

## Complexity and edge cases

O(n·wordCount·wordLength) time and O(wordCount·wordLength) temporary space; straightforward baseline.
